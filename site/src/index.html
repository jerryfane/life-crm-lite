Six steps, about twenty minutes.

You only do the first two. Claude guides you through the rest, one thing at a time.

  1. Start

    Copy the instructions. In Claude, create a Project called life-crm and paste them as its instructions. The clicks are below.

  2. Talk

    Talk for 2–3 minutes about what matters to you now. Dictation makes it easy: see how.

  3. Answer a few questions

    It asks about what’s still missing, one question at a time, with options to pick from. Usually 3 to 6 questions.

  4. Connect Google Drive and Sheets

    Not connected yet? It walks you through it, step by step. Already connected? It checks which account to use.

  5. Save

    It creates your folder in your Google Drive: your sheet, a subfolder per area, and a short guide.

  6. Open your dashboard

    Your areas in a sidebar, the year at a glance, your timeline and what to do now. How.

Every day after: in the same Project, say what happened (“I booked the painter”) or ask “what should I do today?”. It updates your sheet. The sheet in your Drive is the real data.

Talk, don’t type.

Speaking is faster, and you’ll say more. Two or three minutes is plenty. Messy is fine.

What to talk about

  • What matters to you right now, and why.
  • Which areas you want help with: work, studies, money, health, home… You don’t need all of them.
  • For each area: the goal, what’s done, what’s in progress, what’s stuck, who’s involved, where the documents are, and any dates.
  • How should I show up for you? A kick in the butt, a caring approach, a motivational line, or nothing.

Set up Claude.

Make the Project, then connect Google Drive and Google Sheets so Claude can make your folder and sheet. The skill also guides you in the chat, when it needs it.

Make the Project

  1. In the left sidebar of claude.ai, click Projects.
  2. Click + New Project, name it life-crm, and create it.
  3. Click Set project instructions, paste the instructions, and click Save instructions.

Turn on artifacts (once)

Go to Settings › Capabilities and turn on Code execution and file creation. Claude needs it to download your dashboard and show it as an artifact.

Connect Google Drive and Google Sheets

  1. Go to Customize › Connectors.
  2. Find Google Drive and click Connect. Sign in to your Google account and allow access.
  3. Do the same for Google Sheets.

Let Claude create files

Out of the box, Google Drive's tools are switched off, so Claude couldn't make your folder or your sheet.

  1. In Customize › Connectors, click Google Drive.
  2. Set Read-only tools to Always allow.
  3. Under Write/delete tools, allow (the ✓) Create file and Update file. Share file and Trash file can stay on “Needs approval”.
  4. Click Google Sheets and set get_values, update_values, get_spreadsheet and update_spreadsheet to Always allow.

Turn them on in your chat

In the chat, click + (bottom left of the message box) › Connectors, and switch on Google Drive and Google Sheets. Claude needs both.

Editing Google Sheets is a beta in Claude, on the website and the desktop app.

If something goes wrong

If Claude says it can’t read the instructions: in the chat, ask it to run curl -sL {{SKILL_RAW_URL}} and follow that.

Use a different Google account

  1. Go to Customize › Connectors and click Google Drive under “Your connectors”.
  2. Click Disconnect, then Connect, and sign in with the other account.
  3. Do the same for Google Sheets.
