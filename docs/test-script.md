Goal: run the skill once in Claude and once in ChatGPT, as a participant would. Note anything that breaks (a screenshot plus one line) and send it on gram. I fix it Saturday.

Room page: https://life-crm-lite.jerryfane.com

## A. Claude (Pro), about 20 min
1. On the room page, open the **Claude** tab and follow "Make the Project": create a new Project, paste the skill (use the **Copy the skill** button) into its instructions, and add `template.html` to its files.
2. Start a chat in that Project. Say "Hi".
3. Answer the plan question: **Claude Pro**.
4. Talk: paste the Maya transcript from the room page (Examples → Maya), or talk about your own week for 2 minutes.
5. Answer its follow-up questions. **Check:** one question at a time, with options, 3–6 questions, not only about dates.
6. Google Drive: follow what it says. **Check:**
   - it names your Google account and asks you to confirm;
   - a **Google Sheet** called "<Name>'s life CRM" appears in your Drive (a real Sheet, not an .xlsx);
   - it has the tabs Timelines, Steps, Settings, Collections, People, plus one per list.
7. The page. **Check:** it shows as an artifact, with the tone line, then the timeline, then the matrix.
8. In the same chat, say: "I booked the dentist for 3 November." **Check:** the sheet changes first, then the page.
9. Start a **new chat** in the same Project and ask: "What should I do today?" **Check:** it reads the sheet and answers in the tone you chose.

## B. ChatGPT (Plus), about 15 min
Same as A, using the **ChatGPT** tab. Also check:
- the Google Drive connect steps match what you see;
- whether creating the Sheet works in a normal chat, or only after switching on **Work**;
- the page appears as one HTML block, and **Preview** opens it.

## C. Free plans, about 10 min (if you have a free account, or a friend's)
- **ChatGPT Free:** it must say "reduced version… full experience needs a paid plan", give the data block, and send you to the viewer. In the viewer: paste the block, then **Download my sheet (.xlsx)**. In Drive: upload it, open it with Google Sheets, then **File → Save as Google Sheets**.
- **Claude Free:** does the Project accept the instructions and template.html? Does the **Google Sheets** connector show up? How far do you get before the usage limit?
