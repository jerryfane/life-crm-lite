# Maya, medical student

Fictional person (the same Maya as in the life-crm demo). About 2½ minutes of dictation, recorded on Sunday 11 October 2026. Result: [`data.json`](data.json) (the data block) and [`crm.xlsx`](crm.xlsx) (the same data as a life-crm sheet, made with `python3 tools/convert.py to-xlsx`).

## Follow-up questions (one at a time)

1. **Dr. Navarro hasn't replied. Can you start writing the paper before she confirms?**
   Options: *Yes, I'll start now* · *No, I'll wait for her* · *Not sure*
   **Answer:** Yes, start Monday the 12th and aim to finish by 15 December. If she's still silent by Friday the 16th, ask her in person.
2. **When should the letter request forms go to Dr. Grant and Dr. Chen?**
   Options: *This week* · *By 20 October* · *Before Christmas*
   **Answer:** By 20 October.
3. **The licensing exam: do you have a date in mind?**
   Options: *A specific day* · *Sometime in November* · *Not yet*
   **Answer:** Book it sometime in November.
4. **The parking permit: could someone else do it for you?**
   Options: *Only me* · *The department office could* · *Skip it*
   **Answer:** The department office can, if she sends them the form by Thursday the 15th.
5. **The dentist: by when would you like it booked?**
   Options: *This week* · *By the end of October* · *After exams*
   **Answer:** By the end of October.
6. **Where is your program list now?**
   Options: *In a document* · *In my notes app* · *Nowhere yet*
   **Answer:** In her notes app. The assistant moves the three shortlisted programs into a Programs list, and her two papers into a Papers list.

Then the Google Drive step: she confirms `maya.lindqvist@example.com`.

## What ends up in the data

- **Tone:** kick. *"Three Surgery II assignments are due Tuesday. Phone down, case study 2 first."*
- **Areas (4):** Medical school (teal), Research & papers (indigo), Residency 2027 (amber), Health (green).
- **Steps (18):** two periods (the urology rotation, Internal Medicine II) plus two more running periods (the paper write-up, the program list), a month-only date (licensing exam, `2026-11`), one step waiting on someone (Dr. Navarro), one stuck (the case report), one with no date (the reference manager), a weekly repeat (yoga), one done (the two US electives).
- **Lists (2):** Programs (3 rows), Papers (2 rows).
- **People (4):** Dr. Navarro, Dr. Grant, Dr. Chen, Sara.
